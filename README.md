FluentPath Pro 👁️

Your Complete English Learning Platform

FluentPath Pro is an advanced English-learning application designed to help learners improve their English skills from beginner to advanced levels (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2).

The application provides a complete learning experience through interactive lessons, grammar exercises, vocabulary, stories, listening, speaking, writing, memory training, and educational games.

Main Features

1. Personal Learning Profile

- Create a personal account.
- Choose your English level.
- Select what you want to learn.
- Set your learning goals.
- Track your learning journey.

2. English Levels

- A1 – Beginner
- A2 – Elementary
- B1 – Intermediate
- B2 – Upper Intermediate
- C1 – Advanced
- C2 – Proficiency

3. Grammar Academy

- Complete English grammar lessons.
- Tenses and sentence structures.
- Pronouns, prepositions, articles, and conditionals.
- Interactive exercises.
- Tests with answers and explanations.
- Grammar progress tracking.

4. Vocabulary Bank

- Thousands of English words.
- Everyday expressions.
- Vocabulary organized by level and topic.
- Definitions, translations, and examples.
- Personal word collection.
- Unlimited vocabulary expansion.

5. Reading & Stories

- Short and long English stories.
- Stories organized by CEFR level.
- Reading comprehension exercises.
- Questions and vocabulary explanations.

6. Listening Practice

- Listening exercises for different levels.
- Conversations and everyday English.
- Listening comprehension tests.
- Audio-based learning activities.

7. Speaking Practice

- Pronunciation training.
- Everyday conversations.
- Speaking exercises.
- Practical communication activities.

8. Writing Academy

- Sentence-building exercises.
- Paragraph writing.
- Daily writing challenges.
- Writing corrections and feedback.

9. Memory Training

- Memorize English words and sentences.
- Timed memory challenges.
- Short-term memory exercises.
- Track memory performance.

10. English Games

- Vocabulary games.
- Grammar challenges.
- Sentence-building games.
- Multiple-choice questions.
- Daily challenges and rewards.

11. Find Your English Level

- Comprehensive placement test.
- 50-question assessment.
- Automatic level estimation from A1 to C2.
- Detailed results and answer review.

12. Daily Learning Plan

- Personalized daily activities.
- Daily vocabulary practice.
- Grammar exercises.
- Reading and writing tasks.
- Learning reminders.

13. Progress Dashboard

- Track completed lessons.
- Monitor grammar performance.
- Monitor vocabulary growth.
- Review test results.
- Follow your improvement over time.

Application Goals

FluentPath Pro aims to make English learning easier, more interactive, and more effective by bringing essential learning tools together in one platform.

Learn. Practise. Improve.

From your first English words to advanced communication, FluentPath Pro is your personal English training space.
